Project 05 · Data & Civic Tech

Health4All: Mapping Breast Cancer Data in King County

A data-driven project that visualizes breast cancer screening and outcomes across King County, spotlighting disparities in access so communities and decision-makers can advocate for more equitable health resources.

Type
Team project · Data visualization
Region
King County, WA
Deliverable
Interactive web tool + final presentation
01

Overview

Health4All is a civic-technology project focused on making breast cancer data for King County easier to understand and act on. Public health data exists, but it is often scattered, dense, and hard for everyday community members and advocates to navigate.

Our goal was to bring that data together into a clear, interactive experience that highlights where gaps in screening, access, and outcomes exist, so that the people closest to the problem can use the data to push for change.

02

What we focused on

We centered the project on equity-- not just showing the numbers, but revealing the disparities behind them.

Screening rates Access to care Geographic disparities Health equity Community advocacy
  • Make breast cancer data approachable for non-technical audiences.
  • Map outcomes geographically across King County to expose where disparities concentrate.
  • Give advocates and decision-makers evidence they can point to when requesting resources.
03

Interviews, surveys & user feedback

To make sure the tool actually served the people it was meant for, we grounded the design in research rather than assumptions. Throughout this project, we conducted over 30 interviews and 60 surveys with potential users, then ran their feedback back into the product as it evolved.

  • Ran interviews and surveys to learn how community members, advocates, and decision-makers actually look for and use health data.
  • Used those insights to shape which metrics to surface and how to present them clearly for non-technical audiences.
  • Gathered user feedback on early versions of the tool and iterated on the maps, navigation, and wording to make the data easier to interpret.

This feedback loop kept the project focused on real need, prioritizing clarity and usefulness over simply displaying as much data as possible.

The outcome

Turning public health data into a tool for change.

Health4All takes data that usually lives in spreadsheets and reports and turns it into something communities can actually explore. By mapping breast cancer outcomes across King County, the project makes disparities visible and gives advocates a clearer case for where resources are needed most.
