halle_art

Art fills every space in my education and life.

I spent most of my childhood doing ballet. My mom tells me that I fell in love with ballet the second I saw those pointe shoes in Barbie's Twelve Dancing Princesses. I also taught myself how to draw; colored pencil portraits became my creative outlet after I quit ballet in high school. I also love photography and videography, and I grew up in a musical household, so my siblings, mom, and I all play instruments.

I taught myself how to draw when I was in middle school. I completed my first "realistic" portrait in 2016, when I was about 14. It quickly became my form of expression, and I eventually wrote my college application essay about my art journey. You can read it down below.

My grandma gifted me a watercolor set for Christmas 2017, and it wasn't until my first road trip that I actually took it out and used it. It has traveled with me around California, on my first solo trip (to NYC), my study abroad in Copenhagen, and my post grad adventures.
