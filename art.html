Art fills every space in my education and life.

My mom tells me I fell in love with ballet the second I saw those pointe shoes in Barbie's Twelve Dancing Princesses. I spent much of my childhood in the studio, but when I quit in high school, drawing filled that space. I taught myself in middle school through Disney character sketches, and by 14 I was finishing my first realistic portrait. It became my main form of expression, and eventually the subject of my college application essay (a piece I still share with friends heading into their own applications.).

My grandma gifted me a 12-color watercolor set for Christmas 2017, but it sat essentially untouched until my first road trip. Since then, it's been around California, New York on my first solo trip, Copenhagen for my semester abroad, and through my post-grad adventures.

Creativity has always been a family value. My siblings, mom, and I all play instruments, and photography has become a shared hobby as well. Videography is something I've gotten into on my own, I always take my camcorder on trips.
